What Is Bad Optics?!

A good question, little simpletons, but you should really be asking HOW is Bad Optics. The answer is, we're fine, thanks for asking.

To answer your first question, Bad Optics is a progressive-post-art-punk-whatever band from Seattle, WA. If you want, you can call their music "shamewave." They don't, but you can. Formed in 2018 by singer/guitarist/primary songboy Christian J. Hidell and drummer Joshua Ihler, the band has been releasing music, playing shows, and touring ever since. The current lineup consists of Hidell, Ihler, guitarist Alex Warton, and bassist Linden Crumrine. Their music draws on elements of punk, industrial, cultic folk, and anything weird.

In 2025 the band release their latest full-length record, THE BOUNDED WORLD, based on Christian millenarian cults, "rationalist" tech freaks, The Picture of Dorian Gray, and mid-century-modern furniture. In addition to their usual cacophonous twin-guitar sound and paranoid songwriting, THE BOUNDED WORLD features MORE melodies, MORE vocal harmonies, MORE synthesizers, and a glockenspiel. Get ready for fun!

In 2026, the band will be releasing a series of singles culminating in an EP known as DEATH CULT BLUES and embarking on their first east coast US tour. You can listen to the first track NOW.
